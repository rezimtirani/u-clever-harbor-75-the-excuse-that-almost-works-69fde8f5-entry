One line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week: a pipe burst, a flooding kitchen, and a plumber on site till noon — specific, boring, and almost believable.

> Sorry boss, a pipe burst and my kitchen's flooding — plumber's here till noon, I can't leave it.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the excuse to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 140 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line of at most 20 words.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
